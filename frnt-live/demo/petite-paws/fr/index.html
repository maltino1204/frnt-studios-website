Cleo

We were very happy with Lea’s time with Cleo – from the moment we reached out to her until we met up afterwards to get our keys and discuss how things went during her stay. She was very responsive to all of my messages, we were able to make introductions very easily, and Lea was great at answering my questions. We appreciated her being able to uproot her nights and stay here with our cat so she didn’t get lonely as she’s a very personable cat who doesn’t do well on her own for very long. The photos and updates Lea sent often on how she was doing were very nice, and I’m impressed that she was able to coax Cleo out from hiding spots so quickly – she’s very shy! She also did a great job cleaning up after herself and she was thoughtful enough to ask if it was ok to use our things in the kitchen to cook while she was here. That is exactly what we want from a pet sitter – communicative, trustworthy, and very caring of our cat’s well-being. Thank you Lea, looking forward to the next time we have you over to help with our pets!
