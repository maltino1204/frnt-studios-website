# SOMA: Competitive Analysis

*Agent 1 (Product & UX Strategy), research as of 30 Oct 2026*

## 0. Method and limits

- Direct fetches of the competitor sites (getblys.com, selfkea.de, jeff.services, everywayz.de, welladom.com, start-up.ro) did not resolve from the research container. **All findings come from search-engine snippets** of the competitors' own pages, help centres, app-store and review listings, job ads, startup databases and press. I could not walk through any booking funnel myself.
- **Blys:** most detailed policy data comes from its global help centre, which is mainly about Australia and the UK. Treat it as *Blys group practice*, not verified German terms.
- **EveryWayz:** I could not verify that a beauty/wellness product exists under this name (see §5).
- "(inferred)" marks my own reasoning. Anything not found is marked **not verified**. No figures are estimated unless they are labelled illustrative.

## 2. Blys (getblys.com)

**Positioning & audience.** "The fastest, easiest and most secure way to book a massage" in Germany. It covers Berlin districts (Mitte, Kreuzberg, Prenzlauer Berg and more) with "independent massage therapists" ([Berlin page](https://getblys.com/default/locations/mobile-massage-berlin)). The URL path `/default/locations/` suggests a global template rather than a localised German product (inferred). I found no Blys results for München or Hamburg.

**Discovery & booking.**
- Clients have two routes. They can be "matched almost instantly with one of the available professionals" ([help](https://help.getblys.com/support/solutions/articles/33000226440-how-do-i-book-)). Or they can enter an address to see available therapists and filter by language, gender and other preferences ([help](https://help.getblys.com/support/solutions/articles/33000293867-how-to-check-available-therapist-in-the-area-)).
- An older UK FAQ says clients *cannot* pick a therapist and the "first available" is assigned ([FAQ](https://getblys.com/uk/faqs)). The product apparently moved from dispatch to choice.
- Providers are notified by SMS, email or the Pro app and accept or decline.
- Berlin page: bookings run 7 days a week, 6:00–24:00, same-day or in advance.

**Trust.** Trustpilot shows 4.5 from about 1,900 reviews, mostly from Australia ([Trustpilot](https://de.trustpilot.com/review/www.getblys.com.au)). The details of provider verification in Germany are **not verified**.

**Payments & fees.**
- No cash. Cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted, and "you won't be charged until your booking is confirmed" (Berlin page).
- A **10% platform fee** applies to confirmed bookings.
- **Peak-hour surcharges** apply in early mornings and evenings, charged pro rata per hour per provider, and there are hotel surcharges ([help](https://help.getblys.com/support/solutions/articles/33000292171)).
- **Cancellation:** the help pages conflict. One lists 20% at 48–72 h and 50% at 24–48 h, plus a 10% processing fee ([article A](https://help.getblys.com/support/solutions/articles/33000226402-what-is-the-blys-cancellation-and-rescheduling-policy-for-clients-)). Another lists 50% at 24–4 h and 100% under 4 h, waived for severe illness or emergency ([article B](https://help.getblys.com/support/solutions/articles/33000226423)).
- Prices: from £68 per 60 min in the UK ([UK](https://getblys.com/uk/)) and from $129 in the US ([US](https://getblys.com/us/services/)). **German prices not found.**

**Supply side.**
- Providers "earn up to 75%" (Berlin page snippet), which implies a take of ≥25% (inferred). They use a separate app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1321744681)).
- A provider who cancels a confirmed booking may owe Blys a fee ([abandonment policy](https://help.getblys.com/support/solutions/articles/33000226384-provider-abandonment-policy)).
- Providers who fall ill carry that risk themselves: "you own your business" ([help](https://blys.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/33000281070-do-i-still-pay-the-blys-platform-fee-if-i-m-unwell-or-unable-to-work-and-complete-a-confirmed-booking)).

**Mobile & retention.**
- There is a client app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1176837013)) and a "Rebook" option for previous therapists (help centre, via snippet).
- Gift vouchers are sold, including bulk corporate vouchers. Snippets of the voucher terms say they are valid for 3 years (not verified for Germany).
- Employers can buy credits for staff ([blog](https://getblys.com/uk/blog/at-home-employee-wellness-benefit-distributed-teams/)). Corporate events are priced by duration and headcount ([corporate](https://getblys.com/en-nz/corporate-wellness/)).
- A referral programme was **not verified**.

**Strengths:** mature operations; matching and choice; peak and hotel pricing; B2B gifting; native apps.
**Weaknesses (Germany):** template presence, English-centric reviews. Add-on fees are risky under PAngV. Cancellation terms are inconsistent. A high take leaves local rivals room on supply (inferred).

## 3. Selfkea (selfkea.de)

**Positioning & audience.** Selfkea is a Bremen-based UG founded in 2026 ([Startbase](https://www.startbase.de/organization/selfkea/)). It is a marketplace for "Mobile Massage, Maniküre, Fußpflege & mehr" at home, the office or a hotel ([home](https://www.selfkea.de/)). Its own pages list Hamburg, Bremen, Hannover, Köln, Düsseldorf, Essen and Kiel; one article claims nationwide coverage ([blog](https://www.selfkea.de/blog/mobile-massage-zu-hause), [trendkraft](https://trendkraft.io/geschaeft/selfkea-launcht-deutschen-marktplatz-fuer-mobile-beauty-und-wellnessdienstleistungen-40388)). It positions massage explicitly as wellness, not medical.

**Discovery.**
- City × category SEO pages such as [/mobile-massage/duesseldorf](https://www.selfkea.de/mobile-massage/duesseldorf) and [/mobile-manikuere/koeln](https://www.selfkea.de/mobile-manikuere/koeln).
- Customers can compare profiles, prices, reviews and available times.
- A content blog also works as provider acquisition ([nail-tech guide](https://www.selfkea.de/blog/mobile-nageldesigner-selbststaendig-ohne-studio)).

**Booking journey.** Request → provider accepts → online payment → the service is confirmed on site with a **PIN or QR code**. Booking and payment run only through the platform (selfkea.de pages, via snippet). There is **no instant booking** in the material found.

**Trust.** All providers pass **Stripe Identity** checks before activation (Startbase). Customers can report problems **within 48 h** after the service. Insurance and qualification checks are **not verified**.

**Payments & cancellation.**
- Card, Apple Pay, PayPal and Klarna are accepted. A "Premium-Zahlart-Gebühr" exists for some payment methods.
- **Flex package:** an optional add-on, reported at **4% of the service price**. It allows free cancellation up to 24 h before, with a full refund. It is explicitly "not insurance".
- If the provider cancels, the service price, travel fee, Flex fee and premium payment fee are refunded.
- Travel is set per provider as a flat fee, a per-km rate or a free radius.
- Price guidance from Selfkea: Maniküre 35–55 €, Shellac 45–70 €, Gel 70–110 € ([blog](https://www.selfkea.de/blog/mobile-manikuere-zu-hause)).

**Provider model.** **5% of the service price plus VAT**, charged only on appointments that took place. No fixed costs, minimum term or setup fee. The blog cites provider earnings of 30–90 € per appointment.

**Strengths:** very low take rate; clean German SEO structure; local payment methods; PIN proof of service; KYC.
**Weaknesses:** brand new, and its footprint misses Berlin, München and Frankfurt. No instant confirmation. Paid add-ons put flexibility behind a paywall. A 5% take is unlikely to fund paid acquisition, the Glow2Go trap (inferred).

## 4. jeff (jeff.services)

**Positioning & audience.**
- "Mobile Beauty- & Wellnessbehandlungen … Unsere Experten kommen samt Equipment direkt zu dir nach Hause!" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/jeff.services)). The brand uses **"du"**.
- The operator is jeff technologies GmbH, Düsseldorf, founded 2020. It serves homes or hotels ([job ad](https://join.com/companies/jeff/16282890-teilzeit-mobile-fusspfleger-in-kosmetiker-in-duesseldorf-und-umgebung-bis-18h-woche)).
- It is a separate business from the Spanish "Jeff" super-app ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/brief/jeff-spanish-services-app-acquisitions/)).
- The core is (medical) foot care and mani/pedi, with dense NRW city pages (Köln, Meerbusch, Kaarst, Grevenbroich, Mülheim).
- A review about a care-dependent father suggests a strong segment of seniors and people needing care (inferred) ([Köln](https://jeff.services/en/t/koln/pedicuremedicalandmanicure)).

**Booking & pricing.** Customers see availability and book online (job ad). Prices are **all-inclusive: "Preis für Hausbesuch inkl. Anfahrt"** ([Meerbusch](https://jeff.services/de/t/meerbusch/pedicuremedical)):

|---|---|

- Surcharges may apply for extra effort (very thick nails, heavy callus).

**Supply model.** Job ads offer Minijob, part-time and full-time roles for "Mobile Fußpfleger:in / Kosmetiker:in" ([medi-karriere](https://www.medi-karriere.de/job/stellenanzeige-fußpfleger-in-unterrather_stra_e_106_40468_düsseldorf-augenheilkunde-f540ea752bec821ee93f0eb3d38c2738/)). Staff document treatments in an "Experten-App". This points to an **employment / managed-service model**, not an open marketplace (inferred).

**Trust & retention.**
- Ratings: Trustpilot 4.5 from about 274–288 reviews; a self-reported 4.9 from 174 on city pages; [werkenntdenbesten](https://www.werkenntdenbesten.de/e/113393331/fusspflege/koeln/jeff-mobile-fusspflege-und-kosmetik-koeln-bewertungen.html) 4.90 from 142 (Köln).
- The [voucher shop](https://shop.jeff.services/) sells **prepaid bundles**, for example 3× med. Fußpflege incl. Fußmassage for 192 €.

**Strengths:** transparent all-in pricing; consistent quality through employment; recurring foot-care demand every 4–6 weeks (inferred); prepaid bundles; local SEO.
**Weaknesses:** employment is slow and costly to scale (inferred); narrow, non-premium category mix; NRW-centric; little sign of choosing an individual pro.

The commission rate, average order value, repeat rate and CAC were **not published**.

### Operational lessons for SOMA

1. **Unit economics: repeat rate decides everything.** Illustrative assumption: a 90 € AOV at a 20% take leaves about 16 € contribution after payment fees. At a 40 € CAC, a customer must book about 2.5 times to repay acquisition. Model contribution per *customer*, and cap paid spend per city until 60-day repeat is proven.
2. **Supply density before geography.** About 30 experts across many categories means thin slots per category (inferred), which is exactly the "friction" Glow2Go named. Open each city × category **only above liquidity thresholds**, with waitlists elsewhere.
3. **Two-sided paid marketing is a cash sink.** Glow2Go paid for experts *and* clients. Acquire supply through SEO and content (as Selfkea does), referrals between pros and fair commission; buy demand only where supply exists.
4. **Disintermediation** is not named in the sources. But freelancers plus home visits plus repeat-heavy categories carry structural leakage risk (inferred). Make repeats cheaper and safer *on* the platform (spec §11).
5. **Quality was not the failure mode.** Customers were satisfied and verification was a selling point. Verification is table stakes, not a growth engine.
6. **Platform build cost** rose at the wrong moment. Ship a narrow v1 (PWA, one flow) with no native apps.

## 9. Patterns: adopt or beat

| Pattern | Seen at | SOMA move |
|---|---|---|
| All-in price incl. travel | jeff | **Beat:** the PLZ is known from the first screen, so search shows each pro's exact total |
| Choose a pro *and* fast matching | Blys | **Adopt** choice in v1; add "Schnellster Termin" sort |
| Paid flexibility add-on (Flex) | Selfkea | **Beat:** free cancellation up to 24 h is included |
| Platform fee plus surcharges | Blys | **Beat:** no customer fees in v1 |
| PIN/QR proof of service | Selfkea | **Adopt** as a 4-digit "Start-Code" |
| 48 h complaint window | Selfkea | **Adopt**, tied to the SOMA guarantee and the payout hold |
| KYC via Stripe | Selfkea | **Adopt and extend:** insurance, trade registration, qualifications, Führungszeugnis |
| City × category SEO pages | Selfkea, jeff | **Adopt** at `/behandlungen/[kategorie]/[stadt]` |
| Prepaid bundles, vouchers, corporate credits | jeff, Blys | **v1.1**: Gutscheine, 5er-Pakete; corporate via lead form |
| Lowest take rate (5%) | Selfkea | **Beat on value:** tiered commission, lower for repeat clients |

## 10. Key opportunities for SOMA

1. **Own "premium plus trust" in Germany.** No verified rival combines premium positioning, instant booking and German-grade verification across the top-6 cities. Selfkea is regional and request-only; jeff is NRW foot care; Blys is a global template.
2. **True total price on the results list.** Collect the PLZ first and show "94 € inkl. Anfahrt" per pro. It is PAngV-safe and clearly better than "ab" prices plus surprises at checkout.
3. **Instant confirmation as the default.** Selfkea is request-first. "Sofort bestätigt" on the first screen of results converts better (inferred) and fits the "Uber" promise.
4. **No add-on fees.** No Flex fee, no platform fee and no payment-method fee. Free cancellation until 24 h is the headline promise.
5. **Tiered commission against leakage.** A higher rate on SOMA-sourced first bookings and a lower rate on repeats and pro-referred clients make staying on the platform the rational choice.
6. **Verification as a visible product feature.** Badges for ID, Gewerbe, Haftpflicht, qualification and Führungszeugnis. Haare also needs the Handwerksrolle entry, a gap rivals don't advertise.
7. **Liquidity-gated launch.** Open a city and category only above supply thresholds; otherwise run a waitlist. This is the direct lesson from Glow2Go's thin supply and paid-growth dependence.
8. **Hotel and office as distinct flows.** Room number and reception name for hotels; company invoice data for offices. Hotel concierge partnerships give low-CAC demand (inferred). Blys already prices hotels separately.
9. **Cadence-based rebooking.** Nails every 3 weeks, lash refills every 2–3, massage every 4. One-tap rebooking with the same pro turns satisfied customers into recurring revenue, the thing Glow2Go lacked.
10. **Supply acquisition through content.** Copy Selfkea's "selbstständig ohne Studio" playbook at `/fuer-profis`, plus an earnings calculator and pro-to-pro referrals.
11. **Gifting.** Vouchers and bundles are proven at jeff and Blys and match German gifting habits (Christmas, birthdays). Ship in v1.1.
12. **Wellness-safe copy as a feature.** Strict HWG-compliant language (no healing claims) and protected titles ("Podologe", "medizinische Fußpflege", "Physiotherapeut") used only by verified holders. This keeps SOMA safe as it scales SEO content.
13. **Reliability scoring.** Rank on acceptance rate, response time and cancellations, not only stars. Most ratings are already 4.5–4.9, so stars don't differentiate.
